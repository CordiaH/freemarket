# Freemarket — Individual Contributor License Agreement

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual CLA v2.0, with
Sections 8 and 9 added for successor-entity assignment.

**This draft has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** Have one look at it before
you accept the first contribution — particularly Sections 8 and 9, which are
the non-standard part and the whole reason the document exists. Fixing a CLA
retroactively means re-contacting every contributor who ever signed the old
one.

This section exists so that stewardship of the Project can pass to a
non-profit without requiring every past contributor to be located and asked
again. It does not permit assignment to a for-profit acquirer.

This section permits the Project to move forward as licenses evolve. It does
not permit the Project to be made proprietary. If a future Steward wished to
close the source, this agreement would not allow it, and Your Contributions
could not be used that way.
